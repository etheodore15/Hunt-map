Tap "Add note", then tap the map. Drag a pin to move it; tap it to edit. Notes are saved in this browser.

Trip logs

Records your GPS track while on. Stopping saves the trip automatically — then tap its name to zoom, ✏ to rename, 📝 for a note, the swatch to recolour, 👁 to hide/show, 🗑 to delete.
For a clean track: keep the screen on with the page in the foreground (the app requests a wake lock, but a locked phone stops GPS updates), and make sure your browser has "Precise location" allowed. Coarse network fixes and impossible jumps are filtered out automatically.
