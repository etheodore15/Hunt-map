In the field
Stopping a log saves the trip automatically. Tap a trip's name to zoom to it; ✏ rename, 📝 note, colour swatch to recolour, tick-box to show/hide, 🗑 delete.
Clean-track tips
Keep the screen on with the app in the foreground (a wake lock is requested, but a locked phone stops browser GPS — the Android app keeps logging in the background). Allow "Precise location". Coarse network fixes and impossible jumps are filtered out automatically.
